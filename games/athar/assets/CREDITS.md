## Local placeholders
- Generated by `games/athar/tools/horror_room_assets.py` and invoked through `python games/athar/tools/horror_room_assets.py`.
- Status: `PLACEHOLDER`; not presented as external photographs or as works by the credited Poly Haven authors.
- Replace by running `python games/athar/tools/fetch_horror_textures.py` then `python games/athar/tools/horror_room_assets.py`.
## Village model geometry
- Generated by `games/athar/tools/horror_village_assets.py` from deterministic procedural geometry.
- Textures are embedded in the GLB files. If dedicated village maps are unavailable, the generator reuses already bundled CC0 Poly Haven maps; any procedural fallback maps remain explicitly marked `PLACEHOLDER` in `asset_manifest.json`.
